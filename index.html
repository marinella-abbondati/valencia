Perfil

Nearly two decades spent moving between languages, systems, and cultures — as a bilingual production manager coordinating international shoots for clients like Volkswagen and Channel 4, as an editor and translator working between Spanish and English, and as a co-founder navigating the daily reality of running a small business across borders.
