Case studies

Planning a complicated trip

Sue lives in Cuba, where sanctions have severely restricted flights in and out. She had spent a month stressing about how to fly to the UK for her annual six-week visit.

In a single session we explored a wide range of routes and options together, then I saw the chosen option through to booking and confirmation.

OutcomeA schedule that suited her perfectly, at the lowest possible cost.

I was in the middle of building works, with an intermittent electricity supply and a very poor wifi connection, and I felt quite overwhelmed by the idea of making a travel plan. Fuel prices had rocketed since the Iran War began, time was running out, and every known route came up way too expensive for my budget.

I contacted Marinella because I just needed someone to do the research for me, to take the weight and worry completely away. And that's exactly what she did! In a couple of hours, and with much humour and patience, she came up with three different options. Once I had chosen one, she helped me through the process until everything was booked, paid for, extra legroom assured, baggage sorted, and I had received a confirmation email from the airline. A huge relief for me.

I would absolutely use her again, and for anything that demands time, a bright and searching brain, patience and understanding. Thanks Marinella.

Sue, Cuba

Resolving a UK property matter, remotely

Harry lives in Cuba, where power cuts and poor connectivity made it impossible to complete an online licensing application for his London rental property before the deadline.

I reviewed the new law, worked through the long list of required information, documents (including Land Registry documents) and photos, and prepared, archived and submitted the whole application on his behalf.

OutcomeConfirmation received the next day that everything had been accepted.

I live in Cuba, where there are so many problems that affect a focussed response to anything complicated: water shortages, electricity outages, storms, poor connectivity. I had a very complex issue to sort with my London rental property, a new licensing law and a very clumsy Housing Department online platform on which to process it all. I just couldn't do it with the poor connection here. I had a deadline I couldn't meet.

Marinella took the whole online bundle. She thoroughly reviewed the new law, then the long list of required information, documents and photos I needed to produce. She copied, archived, uploaded and saw the whole thing through. The next day I received confirmation that all had been received and accepted.

Marinella is very bright indeed, quick to see both the whole picture and then, with equal speed, to identify each stage of the process until finished. She's also a very sweet, kind and empathetic person, which is lovely. I highly recommend her. I couldn't have done this without her.

Harry, Cuba
